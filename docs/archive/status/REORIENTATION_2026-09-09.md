## Loop discipline

- Each iteration names one observable, hypothesis, falsification condition,
  focused experiment, first failure, and next action. Product observables outrank
  capture size, tests passed, source lines, tier counts, and symbol totals.
- Do not add a tier to a unit already demonstrated to compile whole. Classify
  missing link/runtime owners and compose them. If complete compilation is
  blocked, record the actual diagnostic and preserve the complete-owner plan.
- Resolve endian, pointer-width, ABI and ownership defects at their source.
  Do not introduce success stubs, fabricated scene state, or weaker assertions.
- Three iterations of the same experiment shape trigger subsystem reorientation,
  even when different symbols or test counts make each appear new.
- Verify focused behavior first, then affected integration/configuration checks
  and required regression before a stable checkpoint. Do not run broad matrices
  after the focused hypothesis already fails.
- Checkpoint qualified, reproducible, public-safe increments on main under the
  resumed goal's standing authority. Preserve unrelated work and private data.
