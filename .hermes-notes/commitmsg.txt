Android: import the disc on first run, on the device

Show a first-run screen when main.dol or the rels are missing, as the
iPhone and iPad app does. The player picks their own GZLE01 disc image;
it is copied into the game folder and main.dol + rels/ are extracted from
it on the device (disc_import.c, the same importer the other platforms
use). A separate launcher activity owns the flow before SDL starts, so the
game's own lifecycle cannot tear the screen down.
